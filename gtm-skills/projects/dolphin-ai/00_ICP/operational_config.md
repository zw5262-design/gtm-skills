SOURCING METHOD: auto-search free/public sources only, no paid data APIs

SOURCES TO USE, in priority order:
1. LinkedIn — best source: company search, headcount, job posts,
   named contacts at target titles
2. Apollo.io — free credits, contact-level detail once a company is
   shortlisted
3. Crunchbase — funding stage, recent rounds
4. BuiltWith — tech-stack signals, can reveal competitor tools in use
5. GetLatka — SaaS ARR range as a headcount/stage proxy
6. PitchBook — recent VC/PE activity as an additional funding signal
7. Lookalike-model tools (e.g. AI Ark / Ocean / DiscoLike) — only if a
   seed list of best-fit existing customers has been provided; skip
   otherwise
8. StoreLeads — Shopify / DTC /2C-brand company list (size filter required)
9. D7LeadFinder — local 2C SMB (multi-location chains + single stores, location filter)

VOLUME PER RUN: 20 companies/day (matches the daily send capacity)

CONTACT REQUIREMENT: best-effort — never fabricate an email; leave
blank rather than guess

CHANNELS ACTIVE: Email (auto-send via Gmail, capped at 25/day total
across all active cadences, Hot-tier touches prioritized first if the
cap would be exceeded) + LinkedIn (always manual — never auto-sent,
always written to file for manual copy/send)

CADENCE BY TIER:
- Hot: email (framework per trigger_type) → LinkedIn connect (day 2) →
  email bump (day 5) → LinkedIn follow-up (day 7). One email follow-up
  only.
- Warm: email → email bump (day 5). No LinkedIn.
- Cold: single email, no follow-up.

VOICE: direct, low-hype, peer-to-peer — a founder talking to a fellow
practitioner, not a salesperson working a lead. No "hope this finds you
well," no fake urgency, one clear ask per message. Email body 25-45
words ideal, 75 words absolute max, skimmable in under 5 seconds.
Subject lines: 1-3 words, lowercase, casual. CTA: soft/value CTA, never
a hard "book a call" ask on the first touch. Never name a competitor by
name in cold outreach.
